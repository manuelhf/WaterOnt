LLM runs of 31 October (TF-IDF RAG, dense RAG, GraphRAG) and 11 November (full-context LLM),
made while the flat-text pipe descriptions did not state the supply zone or the monitoring
status. Six of the 24 questions (supply zone and monitoring gap) were therefore unanswerable
for the flat-text configurations, and rule R5 could not be applied from the descriptions.
The descriptions were then extended and all LLM configurations rerun together; the paper
reports that rerun (paper/results). Kept for transparency.
