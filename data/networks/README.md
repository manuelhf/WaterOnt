# Networks

Net1/Net3/Net6 ship with WNTR and can be named directly (`--inp Net3`).

`L-TOWN.inp` is the original `L-TOWN_v2_Model.inp` (L-TOWN, KIOS Research and Innovation
Center of Excellence, BattLeDIM 2020), renamed and otherwise unmodified. It is distributed
under the European Union Public Licence v. 1.2; the full licence text is in
`LICENSE-L-TOWN-EUPL-1.2.txt` and must accompany any copy. See `../NOTICE.md`.

Observed leak records (BattLeDIM 2018/2019) can be supplied as CSV with columns
`pipe,date[,mode]` via `--failures` / `--holdout-failures`.
